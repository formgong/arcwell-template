Modern interior design

From sketch to finished room: Arcwell is an interior design studio for apartments, houses, offices and small public spaces. Every project starts with how you live and work, and ends with a home that fits it, not a showroom.

We believe a good interior is not a luxury but the way a modern home works: the right amount of storage, light where you need it, and materials that age well. Our style is calm and minimal, and we happily mix in pieces you already love. We work in the city and travel for the right project.

When do you need an interior designer?

Straight answers about design and renovation

  1. You can't see how everything will fit

    We draw a detailed floor plan with every dimension and use designers' tricks to make room, such as moving a wall a few centimetres so two built-in wardrobes fit back to back.

  2. It's hard to picture the finished rooms

    The design project includes photorealistic views of every room, so you see the result before anything is built, often with ideas you would not have thought of. We can also make a 3D walk-through.

  3. You doubt the design can actually be built

    We design with building in mind and choose materials that fit the budget. During supervision we check the details that make or break a room: joints between materials, tile grout colour, the depth of lit niches.

  4. You don't know where to find good builders

    We work with several crews we trust and help you compare their prices. Complex details need skilled hands, and we know who has them.

  5. You want to save on the fit-out

    We show where saving is safe and where it is not. Spend on what you touch every day: door handles, taps, kitchen fronts and worktops. Save on what you only look at.
